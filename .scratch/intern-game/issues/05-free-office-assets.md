# Free low-poly office assets

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Where can the project get free low-poly 3D assets for an office and its characters? Include toilets and bathroom fixtures. For each source, find out its licence, the file formats (glTF preferred), whether the styles match each other, and whether characters come rigged or animated. Kenney and Quaternius are two places to start.

## Answer

Use Kenney as the base set: the Furniture Kit for the office and bathroom, and Mini Characters for the Intern, Bosses and Colleagues. The Furniture Kit has a toilet, a bathroom sink, a bathtub, desks, a computer screen and a laptop. Mini Characters reportedly has 12 rigged characters with 32 animations each. Both are CC0 and ship as GLB, so Three.js loads them directly and you need no credits. Add Quaternius Ultimate House Interior (CC0, glTF) for extra bathroom pieces; KayKit and Sketchfab are weaker fits because KayKit's free characters are fantasy and Sketchfab styles do not match.
