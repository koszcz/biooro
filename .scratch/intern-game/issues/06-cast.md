# Cast

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01

## Question

Who is in the slice? Decide the one or two Bosses and the two or three Colleagues. For each one, decide their quirk, their role, and their Faction. Then decide what each Faction wants and why the Factions clash.

## Answer

### Factions

The Factions clash over Smart range complaints. One side buries them, and the other escalates them. Neither side is noble: each one is covering itself.

| Faction | Goal | Why | Members |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Launch Team** | Bury Smart range complaints. | The Smart range launch pays their bonus. Bad news would kill the launch. | Pan Zbigniew Pogodny (Boss), Ryszard Szablon, Wiola Ufna |
| **Paper Trail** | Escalate Smart range complaints. | They want written proof that they warned people. When it blows up, someone else gets blamed. | Pani Halina Teczka (Boss), Kamil Przeciek |

- "Launch Team" is the official name, and it has a logo. "Paper Trail" began as a mocking nickname from the Launch Team, and the side adopted it.
- The Launch Team is bigger on purpose. Escalating should feel outnumbered.
- The Intern does not know the Factions exist on Day 1. The Intern discovers them through Tasks by Day 2.
- The Intern can stay neutral, but neutrality drains goodwill from both Factions. Ticket 07 sets the exact cost.
- Nobody switches Faction during the slice.

### Bosses

**Pan Zbigniew Pogodny**: Head of Customer Complaints, the Intern's direct Boss. Leads the Launch Team.
- Never says a negative word. "Problem" becomes "opportunity", and "complaint" becomes "feedback moment".
- Grows visibly calmer as things get worse. The calm turns creepier as Escalation rises.
- His surname means "serene" in Polish.

**Pani Halina Teczka**: Head of Product Safety. Leads the Paper Trail.
- Documents everything and records every conversation "for the file".
- Fears Head office more than anyone does. She wants proof that she warned them.
- She is an ally who would sell the Intern out to stay clean.
- Her surname means "folder" in Polish, with an echo of secret-police files.

### Colleagues

**Ryszard Szablon** (Launch Team): the Customer Complaints veteran.
- Has worked in Customer Complaints for 19 years and has a template reply for everything.
- Wins "case closure" awards by closing cases without reading them.
- Teaches the Intern how to fake Tasks.
- His surname means "template" in Polish.

**Wiola Ufna** (Launch Team): the Smart range believer.
- Uses a Smart range seat at home and quotes the mission statement sincerely.
- Knows small personal things about people and never says how.
- She is the main channel for the sinister leak.
- Her surname means "trusting" in Polish.

**Kamil Przeciek** (Paper Trail): the Product Safety tester.
- Eats lunch in the stairwell and keeps a binder of printed complaints.
- Is right about everything, but nobody believes him because of how he says it.
- Makes escalating feel justified but exhausting.
- His surname means "leak" in Polish, in both the plumbing and the news sense.

### Names and language

- All characters have Polish speaking names. English players will not notice the meanings. Polish players get a hidden joke. Never explain the meanings in game text.
- The Intern addresses Bosses formally, Polish style: "Pan Zbigniew" and "Pani Halina". Colleagues use first names.
- Game text is written in English. The slice ships in English only, but all text must be ready for translation. A Polish version comes after the slice.
