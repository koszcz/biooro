# Company identity

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

What is the toilet equipment company? Decide its name, its product line, its mission statement, and its tone. The tone is either mundane gone absurd or cheerfully sinister. Also decide which department the Intern joins.

## Answer

- **Tone:** mundane on the surface, with sinister things leaking through. The leak grows as Escalation rises. Day 1 feels dull. By Day 5, the Smart range seems to know things it shouldn't.
- **Name:** Biooro.
- **Scale:** a mid-size regional firm. The Intern's floor is one of many. Head office is distant, never appears, and the Bosses fear it.
- **Product line:** ordinary toilets, cisterns, brushes, and seats, plus the new Smart range of seats that measure things. Complaints about the Smart range grow stranger each day.
- **Department:** Customer Complaints. Complaints touch every other department, which gives the Intern reasons to meet Colleagues from several Factions.
- **Secret (writers only):** the Smart range harvests users' data for Head office. The slice never reveals this. Every hint must stay consistent with it. Keep it out of `CONTEXT.md` and out of game text.
- **Mission statement:** "Biooro. Listening to your body, one flush at a time." It reads as wellness fluff early and as a threat later.

Glossary terms added to `CONTEXT.md`: Biooro, Head office, Customer Complaints, Smart range.
