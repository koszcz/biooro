# Biooro

A story-driven office satire game. The player is an intern at a toilet equipment company and tries not to get fired.

## People

**Intern**:
The player character, newly hired at the company.
_Avoid_: Player (in story text), employee, hero

**Boss**:
A superior who hands the Intern Tasks.
_Avoid_: Manager, supervisor

**Colleague**:
A fellow employee at the Intern's level, each one strange in their own way.
_Avoid_: Coworker, NPC, teammate

**Faction**:
A group led by one Boss, with Colleagues who share its agenda. Siding with one Faction angers another. The Intern may stay neutral, at a cost.
_Avoid_: Clique, team, group

**Launch Team**:
The Faction that buries Smart range complaints to protect the launch. Its name is official.
_Avoid_: Bury side, the cover-up

**Paper Trail**:
The Faction that escalates Smart range complaints so blame lands elsewhere. The name began as the Launch Team's mocking nickname.
_Avoid_: Escalate side, whistleblowers

## Work

**Task**:
A piece of work a Boss or Colleague assigns to the Intern. The more Tasks the Intern finishes, the more Tasks the Bosses assign.
_Avoid_: Job, assignment, quest, mission

**Task side**:
The Faction a Task serves, set by whoever assigns it. Some Tasks serve no Faction.
_Avoid_: Alignment, allegiance

**Fallout**:
A Task that returns two days after the Intern fakes one. It takes longer than the original.
_Avoid_: Consequence, penalty, callback

**Break**:
An hour the Intern spends away from Tasks to recover Sanity.
_Avoid_: Rest, pause, downtime

**Conversation**:
A talk between the Intern and a Boss or Colleague, from its first line to its last. A Conversation that is cut off midway counts as never held.
_Avoid_: Chat, dialogue, talk

**Task choice**:
How the Intern handles a Task: do it, fake it, delegate it, or sabotage it. Doing, faking, and delegating finish the Task. Sabotage does not.
_Avoid_: Action, option

**Pressure**:
One of four visible measures of the Intern's standing: Performance, Launch Team goodwill, Paper Trail goodwill, and Sanity. If any Pressure runs out, the Intern is fired.
_Avoid_: Meter, stat, health

**Performance**:
The Pressure that tracks how Pan Zbigniew rates the Intern's output.
_Avoid_: Boss patience, productivity, score

**Goodwill**:
The Pressure that tracks how one Faction feels about the Intern. Each Faction has its own goodwill.
_Avoid_: Reputation, favour, loyalty

**Sanity**:
The Pressure that tracks how much more of Biooro the Intern can take.
_Avoid_: Stress, health, morale

**Workday**:
The fixed number of hours the Intern has each day. Each Task choice spends some of them.
_Avoid_: Turn, energy, action points

**Firing**:
The failure state. The Intern loses the job and the run ends. Each Pressure has its own Firing, with its own scene.
_Avoid_: Game over, death

## World

**Escalation**:
The office becoming more absurd over time. It rises each day and rises further with choices that stir up the Smart range story. The player never sees it as a number; the office itself shows it.
_Avoid_: Chaos level, weirdness

**Biooro**:
The toilet equipment company where the Intern works. It is a mid-size regional firm that looks wholesome on the surface.
_Avoid_: The company, the firm, the corporation

**Head office**:
Biooro's distant headquarters, which the Bosses fear. It never appears on the Intern's floor.
_Avoid_: HQ, management, upstairs

**Customer Complaints**:
The department the Intern joins. It handles complaints and returns about Biooro products.
_Avoid_: Support, customer service, helpdesk

**Product Safety**:
The department that tests Biooro products. It shares the Intern's floor and leads the Paper Trail.
_Avoid_: QA, quality control, compliance

**Smart range**:
Biooro's new line of toilet seats that measure things. Complaints about it grow stranger as Escalation rises.
_Avoid_: Smart toilets, smart line, IoT products
